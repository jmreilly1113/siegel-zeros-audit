B. nanoda stack size (nanoda_lib)
STATED:
- README at 3a24072: configuration is a JSON file only; its documented options are export path / use_stdin,
  permitted_axioms, unpermitted_axiom_hard_error, unsafe_permit_all_axioms, nat_extension, string_extension,
  pp_* options, declar_sep, print_success_message. No stack option.
- src/lib.rs:23 (3a24072 and current master): STACK_SIZE = 16_777_216, used by src/tc.rs .stack_size for every
  checker thread. ulimit -s affects only the main thread (issue #55: "The main thread has a separate stack limit").
- Issue #55 (open, nealmcb, 2026-10-15), openai/math fd4aeeb, same nanoda/lean4export commits as ours: 15 of 416
  configs fail. The RoundTables group (DirectedFeedback, UniqueGamesTheorem, BinPackingGap, VertexCover,
  IndependentSets, MinUncut, PerfectCompleteness, OptimalMaxCut), Laughlin, LaughlinPlanar, LaughlinFock,
  Problem355, KMedianThreshold, SquareDifference, ContinuumCoulombHardness. Larger stacks (1 GiB, 16 GiB) did not
  help: the backtrace is whnf/try_reduce_nat alternating ~10^5-10^6 times (a reduction that does not make
  progress), and at 16 GiB UniqueGamesTheorem ran to 285 GiB and panicked.
- Issue #49 (closed 2026-10-14): do_nat_bin reduced its second argument before checking the first; "nanoda cannot
  check that proof [Unique Games] with any stack size". Fixed by 0e520fd / e8a9606.
- #55 comment (nealmcb, 2026-10-16): on master f67473b, 14 of the 15 now pass (under 10 min, under 25 GiB);
  SquareDifference still overflows (def_eq / lazy_delta_step recursion).
- #57 (open) and PR #58 (merged 2026-10-19, master 9d6964f) remove a has_fvars check; a #57 comment reports this
  brings the #55 overflow back on ContinuumCoulombHardness.
- nanoda master is 16 commits ahead of 3a24072 (compare 3a24072...master).
COMPARED with ours:
- Of our 7 nanoda crashes, 5 are named in #55 (OptimalMaxCut, UniqueGamesTheorem, VertexCover, BinPackingGap,
  Laughlin). KaplanskyFinitelyPresented and GroupRingDeterminant are not named there.
- Two configs still to run tonight are in the #55 RoundTables group: IndependentSets (batch 6) and
  PerfectCompleteness (batch 7). INFERRED: expect nanoda stack overflows there too (ACCEPT_LEAN_ONLY if Lean accepts).
- Our own 512 MiB-stack nanoda copy still overflowed on OptimalMaxCut (results/2026-10-12-nanoda-bigstack-diag/).
INFERRED: a retry with stock 3a24072 under ulimit -s cannot change the checker-thread stack, and a larger
STACK_SIZE is reported not to help these proofs. The upstream fix is in later nanoda commits (f67473b).
