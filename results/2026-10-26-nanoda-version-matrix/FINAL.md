# nanoda version matrix: final summary (2026-10-26, UNOFFICIAL)

**Scope.** These are unofficial nanoda builds run on exports of openai/math fd4aeeb. The official tables are unchanged: the 189 referenced configs, and the 227 extended-replay configs with nanoda 3a24072 at 16 MiB through comparator.

**Builds** (MANIFEST.txt has sources, patch and binary hashes):
- B1: 3a24072 with a 1 GiB stack.
- B2: master 37cb87b, stock (16 MiB).
- B3: 37cb87b with nealmcb's let fix and Nat.succ guard, at 64 MiB.

**Run settings.** Each run was direct, 4 threads, 30 min timeout, 16G cap on RAM plus swap, one at a time, under the guard. An OOM at the cap is a resource outcome, not a verdict on the proof.

**Sources.** Cells are from matrix-v3.tsv, the version that supersedes matrix.tsv and matrix-v2.tsv; every cell's log is there. Tags: C = COMPUTED here, S = STATED by nealmcb.

## Three failure classes

1. **Recursion in 3a24072 that more stack does not cure; fixed by f67473b.**
   - Configs: the RoundTables group, the Laughlin group, and (from step 5) KMedianThreshold and Catalan.
   - Evidence (C): they fail on B1 by growing to the cap, and pass on f67473b, B2 and B3.
   - The recursion pattern was measured only by nealmcb's gdb backtrace, on RoundTables. Membership is INFERRED from that behaviour for Laughlin, KMedianThreshold and Catalan.
2. **Deep but finite Nat recursion.** f67473b made it shallower; PR #58 made it deeper again.
   - Configs (C): the three Kaplansky/GroupRing configs. They fail at 16 MiB on 3a24072 and on master, and pass at 64 MiB and at 1 GiB.
   - STATED (nealmcb): stack need grows with the literal size, measured for MatchingFPRAS, ContingencyTables and LogspaceEquality. It is INFERRED for our three.
   - Consequence: no fixed stack size guarantees acceptance of future configs.
3. **The let bug: SquareDifference.**
   - C: it fails on 3a24072, f67473b and stock master, and passes on B3.
   - STATED: nealmcb shows the let fix alone is enough, and ammkrn suggests a missing alpha-equivalence check (#55). #62 is open.
   - B3 also includes the Nat.succ guard, so these runs do not separate the two changes.
   - ContinuumCoulombHardness is flagged: STATED only. It may be class 1 on 3a24072 and need the Nat.succ guard on master.

## Step 5 finding

- **Both memory deferrals were nanoda 3a24072, not the proofs.**
  - KMedianThreshold: 3a24072 reached 19.84 GB and was still growing. On master it needs 25 s and about 1.1 GB.
  - Catalan: 3a24072 reached 19.6 GB and was still rising. On master it needs about 550 s and 4.6–5.0 GB.
  - Lean's kernel accepts both here, through the shipped-config comparator: KMedianThreshold in 369 s and Catalan in 2,234 s, both under 7.3 GB.
- **Status:** they now read "both kernels accepted here; nanoda from unofficial tool set B3". Their official outcome stays NOT_CHECKED_HERE, because the pinned tool set did not finish.
- **ContinuumCoulombHardness could not be built here.** The guard stopped its build at 4 threads and at 2 threads, so it is permanently deferred, with nealmcb's results STATED.

## Sandbox change (results/2026-10-26-network-check/)

- **The old filter.** It was `RestrictAddressFamilies=~AF_UNIX AF_INET AF_INET6 AF_NETLINK AF_PACKET`, and it blocked IP and Unix sockets.
  - It left three families open: AF_VSOCK, where a connection to the Windows host succeeded; AF_PPPOX; and AF_ALG.
  - The first SiegelZeros run, on 2026-10-08, had only `~AF_UNIX`.
- **The new filter.** From 2026-10-26 16:40 the deny list names all 45 families of kernel 5.15.167.4 (`none` is rejected by systemd-run).
  - t25: all 138 socket() calls fail with errno 97, in user and system units.
  - t26: the tools still work, and lean4export output is byte-identical.
- Network claims across the repo were corrected by appended notes. The published records have drafts only, nothing posted.
